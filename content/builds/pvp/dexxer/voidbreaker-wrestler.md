## Overview

This build is effective at farming in PvM content and doubling as a PK deterrant.  Protect your party while still dishing out PvM damage.

## Aspect Options

- PvM -> Air Armor & Blood Weapon
- PvP -> Void Armor & Void Weapon

## Link Options

- PvM:
    - Melee Special Chance / Special Damage
    - Damage Dealt by Player
    - Melee Damage / Ignore Armor Chance

- PvP
    - Damage Resistance
    - Physical Damage Resistance
    - Effective Parrying Skill

## Codex Use

- Wrestling
    - Solo: You will mostly sit in Dragon Stance when farming. Chi-Strike is a good finisher. Brawl and Haymaker are both viable specials to run.
    - Group: If you are tanking, switch to crab for extra damage resistance, or stay in dragon to show-off

- Parry
    - Solo: Use Shield Bash and situationally switch to Mirror or Warding
    - Group: Use Testudo and keep mobs taunted so they face you for maximum damage resistance
    - PvP: Use Mirror unless you pick up a bleed or disease, at which point switch to Warding
    - Use the Last Stand finisher in all cases.

**Additional Codex info is in the build card**

## Equipment

Full Plate, best ore for your budget.

## Farming/Gameplay

Build can easily face-tank most level 3 dungeons with dodging enemy specials only required in extreme cases. Level 4 dungeons require additional care.

## Additional Notes

Punching faces kicks ass.
