## Overview

This is a flexible gathering template designed to handle both Mining and Lumberjacking on the same character. The combination of gathering skills, Magery, Wrestling, and Spirit Speaking allows the character to collect resources while still having tools available to deal with creatures encountered in the field.

## Aspect Options

**Harvest** is the recommended Aspect for this build.

Additional Aspect choices can be considered depending on whether the character is being used primarily for gathering or PvM.

## Link Options

Chain Links are not a major requirement for this template.

General-purpose gathering, survivability, and PvM links can be added as upgrades.

## Codex Use

- **Summoner's Tome**
  - Provides summoned creatures to help protect you while gathering.
  - Summons can occupy enemies while you continue gathering or prepare to recall.
  - The Tome is particularly useful when working in areas with regular PvM encounters.

**Additional Codex info is in the build card**

## Equipment

- Leather Armor
- Pickaxe for Mining
- Hatchet for Lumberjacking

Prioritize lightweight equipment and useful defensive modifiers while leaving enough carrying capacity for harvested resources.

## Farming/Gameplay

The main purpose of this build is to gather resources efficiently while maintaining enough utility to deal with the PvM threats encountered while farming.

You can switch between Mining and Lumberjacking depending on what resources you need. Magery provides additional utility, while Wrestling helps reduce the chance of being interrupted when attempting to recall.

## Additional Notes

This build is intended as a flexible all-purpose gatherer rather than a specialized combat character.

The ability to gather both wood and ore makes it useful for players who want one character capable of handling multiple resource-gathering jobs.
