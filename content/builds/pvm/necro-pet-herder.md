## Overview

The Necro Pet Herder is a specialized summoner designed around maximizing the effectiveness of summoned creatures.

Herding and Discordance are used to improve your summons while Necromancy provides additional abilities and buffs.

## Aspect Options

Recommended Aspect options include:

- **Death**
- **Eldritch**
- **Command** Book

Command is particularly suited to the summon-focused nature of the build.

## Link Options

Recommended Chain Links include:

- Follower Damage
- Effective Necromancy

These links directly improve the build's primary source of damage.

## Codex Use

- **Necromancy Book**
  - Provides the Necromancy abilities used to support the summons.

- **Summoner's Tome**
  - Provides the creatures that form the core of the build.

- **Wizard's Grimoire**
  - Provides Magery spells and utility.

- **Bard Codex**
  - Provides Discordance and additional Bard abilities.

**Additional Codex info is in the build card**

## Equipment

- Material Spellbook
- Crook
- Standard mage equipment

The Crook supports the Herding component of the build while the Material Spellbook provides useful mana regeneration.

## Farming/Gameplay

Summons form the frontline and primary source of damage.

Herding and Discordance are used to improve their effectiveness while Necromancy provides additional buffs and abilities.

The character remains behind the summons and provides support through Magery and Bard abilities.

## Additional Notes

If mana supply is sufficient through Meditation, mushrooms, Wither, or other sources, Camping can be considered for additional carrying capacity.

Bless from the Wizard's Grimoire can provide an additional damage increase to followers.

## Stats

**STR:** 100, **INT:** 100, **DEX:** 25
