## Overview

The Boss Axeman is a specialized two-handed melee build designed around maximizing damage against bosses.

The build uses Swordsmanship, Tactics, Focus, and Chivalry to provide sustained melee damage while Healing and Anatomy provide additional survivability.

## Aspect Options

Recommended Aspect options include:

- **Madness**
- **Discipline**
- **Blood**
- **War**

These Aspects complement the build's high-damage melee focus.

## Link Options

Recommended Chain Links include:

- Damage to Disease
- Damage to Bosses
- Damage to Poisoned
- Netherzone Damage

Boss Damage should be prioritized when building specifically for boss encounters.

## Codex Use

- **Chivalry Book**
  - Provides healing, buffs, and offensive utility.

- **Swords Codex**
  - Provides the abilities used by the Swordsmanship setup.

- **Healer's Codex**
  - Supports the Healing component of the build.

**Additional Codex info is in the build card**

## Equipment

- Two-handed Axe
- Platemail

Use a two-handed axe to maximize the offensive potential of the build.

Magic damage weapons are particularly valuable because they can increase the effectiveness of the build's Bleed damage.

## Farming/Gameplay

The Boss Axeman is intended primarily for boss encounters rather than general-purpose farming.

Stay engaged with the boss and maintain your Chivalry buffs while using your axe to apply sustained melee pressure.

## Additional Notes

Damage to Disease links are particularly useful when another player is applying disease to the boss.

Healers should focus their support on you when possible, allowing the build to remain aggressive and maximize its melee damage.
