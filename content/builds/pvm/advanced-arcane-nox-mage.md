## Overview

The Advanced Arcane Nox Mage combines the offensive power of an Arcane mage with Poisoning and Necromancy.

The build is designed around kiting enemies while applying poison and using spell damage to maintain pressure.

## Aspect Options

Recommended Aspect options include:

- **Poison** Armor
- **Arcane** Staff

Poison directly supports the build's Poisoning damage while Arcane equipment provides additional spell damage.

## Link Options

Recommended Chain Links include:

- Damage to Diseased
- Damage to Poisoned
- Spell Aspect Chance

Poison Damage links are also a strong option for this build.

## Codex Use

- **Necromancy Book**
  - Provides the Necromancy abilities and buffs used by the build.

- **Wizard's Grimoire**
  - Provides the primary Magery spell selection.

- **Arcane Codex**
  - Provides the Arcane abilities that increase the build's offensive potential.

**Additional Codex info is in the build card**

## Equipment

- Crafted Staff
- Poison Kit
- Poison Aspect equipment
- Arcane Aspect staff

Crafted Staves with Mana Refund and Damage are recommended.

## Farming/Gameplay

The build is designed around kiting and rope casting while applying Poison for additional damage.

Necromancy provides additional offensive tools and buffs while Arcane abilities supplement the build's spell damage.

## Additional Notes

Poison Damage Chain Links are also a strong option.

This is an offensive build with limited defensive investment, so positioning and movement are important when dealing with dangerous PvM encounters.

## Stats

**STR:** 100, **INT:** 100, **DEX:** 25
