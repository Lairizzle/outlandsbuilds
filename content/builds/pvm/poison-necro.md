## Overview

The Poison Necromancer, commonly referred to as the Noxcro, is a high-damage PvM summoner built around Poisoning and Necromancy.

Summoned creatures provide the frontline while Poison, Poison Strike, and Pain Spike provide the primary sources of damage.

## Aspect Options

**Poison** is the recommended Aspect for this build.

Poison directly supports the build's primary damage strategy.

## Link Options

Recommended Chain Links include:

- Damage to Poisoned
- Spell Aspect Chance
- Spell Aspect Modifier

These links reinforce the build's Poison and spell damage.

## Codex Use

- **Necromancy Book**
  - Provides the Necromancy abilities used by the build.

- **Summoner's Tome**
  - Provides summons that tank and deal damage.

- **Wizard's Grimoire**
  - Provides the Magery spells used for additional damage and utility.

**Additional Codex info is in the build card**

## Equipment

- Material Spellbook
- Poison Kit
- Standard mage equipment

The Material Spellbook is recommended for its mana regeneration benefits.

## Farming/Gameplay

Use summoned creatures to tank enemies while applying Poison and using Necromancy abilities for damage.

Poison Strike and Pain Spike provide additional offensive options while your summons occupy enemies.

This build is extremely damage-focused and sacrifices some defensive utility to maximize offensive output.

## Additional Notes

Tracking or Wrestling can be considered for additional defense.

This is one of the more glass-cannon-oriented mage setups, so positioning and avoiding unnecessary damage are particularly important.

**STR:** 100, **INT:** 100, **DEX:** 25
