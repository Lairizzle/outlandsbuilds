## Overview

The Basic Summoner is a straightforward mage designed around letting summoned creatures handle the frontline while you support them with Necromancy and Magery.

It is an accessible starting point for players looking to build a dedicated summoner without relying on complicated mechanics.

## Aspect Options

Recommended Aspect options include:

- **Void**
- **Death**
- **Eldritch**

These provide different ways to improve the offensive and defensive capabilities of the build.

## Link Options

Chain Links are not a major requirement for this build.

General-purpose spell damage, summoning, and survivability links can be added as upgrades.

## Codex Use

- **Wizard's Grimoire**
  - Provides the primary Magery abilities and allows you to contribute direct spell damage.

- **Summoner's Tome**
  - Provides the summons that form the core of the build.
  - Summons act as your frontline and provide additional damage.

**Additional Codex info is in the build card**

## Equipment

- Leather Armor
- Magical Spellbook with high potency

A strong spellbook is useful for increasing your personal spell damage while your summons handle the frontline.

## Farming/Gameplay

Use summoned creatures to tank enemies while you remain at range and cast offensive spells.

Necromancy provides additional abilities and buffs, while Magery gives you a wide selection of direct damage and utility spells.

Wrestling provides additional protection against interruption.

## Additional Notes

The recommended starting skills are 50 Magery, 49 Necromancy, and 1 Meditation.

Training Credits can be used for 50 Wrestling and 50 Evaluating Intelligence.

If you expect to fight other players, Resisting Spells can be considered instead of Focus.

## Stats

**STR:** 100, **INT:** 100, **DEX:** 25
