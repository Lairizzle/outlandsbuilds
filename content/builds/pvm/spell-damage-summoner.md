## Overview

The Spelldamage Focused Summoner is designed around maximizing personal spell damage while summoned creatures provide a frontline.

Necromancy provides additional abilities that enhance the character's offensive potential, while Magery provides the primary source of direct spell damage.

## Aspect Options

Recommended Aspect options include:

- **Void** Armor
- **Eldritch** Book

**Blood** and **Madness** Armor are also viable alternatives.

## Link Options

Recommended Chain Links include:

- Damage to Diseased
- Spell Aspect Chance
- Spell Aspect Modifier

These improve the build's spell damage and Aspect performance.

## Codex Use

- **Necromancy Book**
  - Provides Necromancy abilities and buffs.

- **Summoner's Tome**
  - Provides summons to tank and occupy enemies.

- **Wizard's Grimoire**
  - Provides the primary offensive spell selection.

**Additional Codex info is in the build card**

## Equipment

- Magical Spellbook
- Eminently potent or better is recommended
- Standard mage equipment

A high-quality spellbook is one of the most important offensive upgrades for this build.

## Farming/Gameplay

Summoned creatures should be used to occupy and tank enemies while you focus on casting spells.

Necromancy abilities can be used to improve your offensive capabilities before unloading Magery spells onto your targets.

Wrestling provides additional protection against interruption.

## Additional Notes

Blood Armor and Madness Armor are viable alternatives to the recommended Void and Eldritch setup.

The build is focused heavily on personal spell damage rather than maximizing the strength of the summons themselves.

**STR:** 100, **INT:** 100, **DEX:** 25
