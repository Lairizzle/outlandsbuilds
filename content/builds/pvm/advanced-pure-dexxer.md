## Overview

The Advanced Pure Dexxer is a highly flexible melee template that focuses heavily on weapon damage while retaining Chivalry, Healing, and strong defensive options.

The final playstyle can vary significantly depending on the weapon skill, Aspect setup, and defensive skill chosen.

## Aspect Options

Recommended Aspect options include:

- **Frost**
- **Lightning**
- **Blood**
- **Earth**
- **Fire**
- **Discipline**
- **Holy**

The optimal choice depends heavily on the weapon and intended playstyle.

## Link Options

Recommended Chain Links include:

- Slayer/Dungeon Damage
- Melee Special/Damage
- Swing Speed

If swing speed is not capped, prioritize Swing Speed links.

## Codex Use

- **Chivalry Book**
  - Provides the build's primary healing, buff, and utility abilities.

- **Weapon Skill Codex**
  - Provides abilities for the selected weapon skill.

- **Healer's Codex**
  - Supports the Healing portion of the build.

- **Parry Codex**
  - Provides defensive options when using a shield.

- **Potion Codex**
  - Can be used as an alternative depending on the selected setup.

**Additional Codex info is in the build card**

## Equipment

- Grinding Medium weapon
- Burst two-handed weapon
- Platemail
- Lighter armor for Wrestling variants

The weapon choice should be matched to the selected weapon skill and Aspect setup.

## Farming/Gameplay

This build is intentionally flexible.

The final playstyle changes considerably depending on whether you choose Swords, Maces, Archery, Wrestling, or Fencing. Chivalry provides the common foundation while the remaining skills support damage, survivability, and weapon performance.

## Additional Notes

Arms Lore provides approximately 46% Swing Speed Increase.

Four Silver Swing Speed Increase links can be used to reach the cap when necessary.

The final skill choice can be Parrying, Alchemy, or Resisting Spells depending on the desired setup.

## Stats

**STR:** 100, **INT:** 25, **DEX:** 100
