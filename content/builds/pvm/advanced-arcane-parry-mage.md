## Overview

The Advanced Arcane Parry Mage is a more durable version of the Arcane mage concept.

Rather than relying entirely on kiting, the build invests heavily into Arcane, Wrestling, and Parrying, allowing the character to stand and fight while maintaining strong spell damage.

## Aspect Options

Recommended Aspect options include:

- **Blood** Armor
- **Eldritch** Armor
- **Void** Armor
- **Arcane** Staff

Arcane equipment provides the primary offensive focus.

## Link Options

Recommended Chain Links include:

- Damage to Diseased
- Spell Aspect Chance
- Spell Aspect Modifier

These improve spell damage and Aspect effectiveness.

## Codex Use

- **Necromancy Book**
  - Provides Necromancy buffs and abilities.

- **Wizard's Grimoire**
  - Provides the primary Magery spells.

- **Arcane Codex**
  - Provides the core Arcane abilities.

- **Parry Codex**
  - Provides defensive abilities that allow the mage to stand and fight.

**Additional Codex info is in the build card**

## Equipment

- Crafted Staff
- Plate or other appropriate armor
- Arcane Aspect equipment

Crafted Staves with Mana Refund and Damage are recommended.

## Farming/Gameplay

The build is designed to stand its ground rather than constantly kite enemies.

Necromancy buffs and Arcane abilities provide the offensive foundation while Parrying and Wrestling provide additional defenses.

## Additional Notes

Once the Arcane Codex has been leveled sufficiently, its mana options allow the character to play effectively in plate armor.

Alchemy provides a small amount of additional utility and can be replaced if the character requires a different specialization.

## Stats

**STR:** 100, **INT:** 100, **DEX:** 25
