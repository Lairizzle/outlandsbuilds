## Farming/Gameplay

This build is designed for general PvM farming and performs well against groups of standard enemies.

Your tamed creature should handle a large portion of the incoming pressure while you contribute melee damage and provide additional utility. Keep your pet positioned properly and avoid allowing multiple high-damage enemies to focus you at once.
