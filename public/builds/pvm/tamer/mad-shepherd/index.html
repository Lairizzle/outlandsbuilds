Overview

This is a general-purpose Tamer build designed for PvM and farming. It combines a strong tamed creature with melee damage and utility skills to provide a flexible and relatively inexpensive farming setup.

The build is intended to be easy to get started with while still providing enough room to improve as better equipment, aspects, and chain links become available.

Equipment

The build does not require extremely expensive equipment to get started.

Farming/Gameplay

This build is designed for general PvM farming and performs well against groups of standard enemies.

Your tamed creature should handle a large portion of the incoming pressure while you contribute melee damage and provide additional utility. Keep your pet positioned properly and avoid allowing multiple high-damage enemies to focus you at once.

The build can be used for a variety of farming locations, with the best choice depending on your equipment, pet, and available content.

Additional Notes

This build is intended to be approachable for players who want to get into Tamer farming without making a large initial investment.

The build can be upgraded over time as additional aspects, links, codex abilities, and equipment become available.

Pet selection will also have a significant impact on overall performance, so experiment with different pets to find what works best for your preferred farming content.
