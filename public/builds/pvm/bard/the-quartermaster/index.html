The Quartermaster

A pure support Bard focused on healing, protection, utility, and keeping the party alive.

Last Updated: September 11, 2026
Author: Soren Longhorn
Category PvM
Build Type Advanced
Investment High ◆ ◆ ◆ ◆ ◇
Chain Links Moderate ◆ ◆ ◆ ◇ ◇
Difficulty High

Overview

The Quartermaster is a completely group focused support role. You cannot farm solo with this whatsoever.

Aspect Options

  • PvM -> Lyric

Link Options

See build card.

Codex Use

  • Bard

    • Perfect Pitch — Enhances your Lyric abilities and improves the effectiveness of your support.
    • Refrain — Helps maintain your Bard support effects during extended encounters.
    • Self Taught / Virtuoso — Improves overall Lyric support and efficiency.

  • Wizard’s Grimoire

    • Healing — Provides Cure, Arch Cure, and Greater Heal for reliable direct healing.
    • Protection — Provides Bless, Magic Reflect, Protection, Arch Protection, and Reactive Armor.
    • Resurrection & Utility — Provides Resurrection and Create Food for additional group utility.

  • Potion

    • Bandolier — Improves access to and management of potions.
    • Freedom & Panacea — Provides answers to debilitating effects and helps keep the party operational.
    • Mixed Results — Adds additional potion utility and flexibility.

Equipment

This build will run full plate. Mastercrafted gear at whatever ore your budget will allow.

Farming/Gameplay

This is meant for group PvM only. You cannot run this solo.
